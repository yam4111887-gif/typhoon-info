❓ Frequently asked questions

Who decides, and when is it announced?

City/county mayors decide based on CWA forecasts. Typical announcement times: the night before (around 22:00) or early morning (around 5:00–7:00); afternoon suspensions may be announced around noon.

Do I get paid on a typhoon day?

Per Taiwan's Ministry of Labor (MOL) guidelines: when your workplace or residence is covered by a suspension notice, you may stay home and employers "should" not deduct wages. If you agree to work during a suspension, you should receive extra pay plus transportation support. See MOL (English) for the official rules.

My home county is suspended but my workplace isn't (or vice versa)?

If your workplace, residence, or commuting route passes any area under suspension, the rules generally apply to you. Check with your employer or a labor affairs office.

Typhoon prep checklist

Charge phones and power banks; store 1–2 days of drinking water and food; secure balcony items and windows; keep a flashlight; note the 1999 hotline.
